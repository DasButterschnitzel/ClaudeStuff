## 2. Legal and integration basics (state: Nov 2026)

"Wife of a German resident" is ambiguous. If the husband is a **German citizen**, §28 AufenthG applies; if he is a **foreign resident**, §30. The app should ask once and branch.

- **Right to work:** any residence title allows employment and self-employment unless a law forbids it ([§4a(1) AufenthG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/aufenthg_2004/__4a.html)) [V]. She may work from the day she holds the permit; the card should read "Erwerbstätigkeit erlaubt".
- **Integration course entitlement:** a first residence permit for family reunification (§§28, 30) gives a legal claim to one course ([§44(1) AufenthG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/aufenthg_2004/__44.html)) [V]. **The claim expires one year after the permit is issued** (§44(2)) – a countdown is worth building.
- **Obligation:** she is *obliged* to attend if she cannot communicate in simple German, or lacks B1 when a §28/§30 permit is issued ([§44a(1)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/aufenthg_2004/__44a.html)) [V]. With A1 she will almost certainly be obliged by the Ausländerbehörde. Attendance is taken into account when the permit is extended [K, §8(3)].
- **Course format:** 700 lesson units = 600 language + 100 orientation; special target-group courses up to 900 language units (Integrationskursverordnung) [S]. Final tests: "Deutsch-Test für Zuwanderer" (graded A2–B1) and "Leben in Deutschland" (33 questions; 15 correct passes the orientation course, 17 also counts as the naturalisation test) [S/K].
- **Cost:** €2.29 per unit, about €1,603 for 700 units; exemption on application for people on benefits or in hardship ([BAMF](https://www.bamf.de/DE/Themen/Integration/TraegerLehrFachkraefte/TraegerIntegrationskurse/Organisatorisches/TeilnahmeKosten/Auslaender/auslaender.html)) [S]. Half is refunded if she passes within two years [K – confirm with BAMF].
- **Finding a course:** [BAMF-NAvI](https://bamf-navi.bamf.de/en/) [V]; locally the vhs Kreis Offenbach / vhs Dreieich on the HLL campus, Frankfurter Str. 160–166 [S].
- **Settlement permit:** spouse of a German – normally after 3 years, marriage continuing, and "ausreichende Kenntnisse" = B1 ([§28(2) AufenthG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/aufenthg_2004/__28.html)) [V]. Spouse of a foreigner – general route, 5 years and B1 [K, §9].
- **Naturalisation:** standard 5 years, B1, test, own livelihood ([§10 StAG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stag/__10.html)) [V]. Spouses of Germans: 3 years' residence and 2 years' marriage ([§9 StAG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stag/__9.html)) [V].
- **Before entry:** A1 is required for the visa, with exceptions ([§30(1) AufenthG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/aufenthg_2004/__30.html)) [V] – so she already has a certificate.

**Changed recently – flag in the app and re-check before release**
1. **Feb 2026:** BAMF stopped admitting *voluntary* participants (§44(4)); applications since 1 Dec 2025 were refused. Entitled or obliged spouses are not excluded, but providers report cancelled and postponed courses. [S] [Flüchtlingsrat NRW](https://www.frnrw.de/themen-a-z/sozialleistungen/bamf-setzt-zulassungen-zu-integrationskursen-nach-44-abs-4-aufenthg-fuer-2026-aus.html), [Correctiv](https://correctiv.org/aktuelles/integration-gesellschaft/2026/02/04/bundesamt-fuer-migration-und-fluechtlinge-stoppt-zulassungen-fuer-integrationskurse/)
2. **1 Aug 2026:** sixth amendment of the Integrationskursverordnung (BGBl. 2026 I Nr. 218) reopened a limited quota for voluntary participants. The 2027 course budget is reported to fall from about €1 bn to €600 m. [S] [Paritätischer](https://www.der-paritaetische.de/alle-meldungen/anpassung-der-integrationskursverordnung-zum-01-august-2026/), [Flüchtlingsrat BW](https://fluechtlingsrat-bw.de/aktuelles/lockerung-bei-integrationskurszulassung/)
3. **1 Nov 2026:** an amended test regulation for the Deutsch-Test für Zuwanderer took effect – content not checked. [S]
4. **30 Oct 2025:** the 3-year "turbo" naturalisation was abolished; the spouse rule in §9 StAG is unchanged. [V/S] [Bundesregierung](https://www.bundesregierung.de/breg-de/aktuelles/kabinett-einbuergerung-2350620)

The app must not give legal advice: show the rule, the source, and "ask the Ausländerbehörde or a migration adviser (MBE, free)".

## 4. Top 12 feature recommendations (ranked for this user)

1. **Letter decoder (offline).** Pick the sender or logo, see "what this is, is it urgent, what to do", plus a glossary of Frist, Bescheid, Mahnung – letters cause the most fear and dependence.
2. **Say-it cards with audio.** Ready sentences for doctor, pharmacy, phone appointment, town hall, bakery; show-the-screen mode – unlocks independence at A1.
3. **First-90-days checklist with countdowns.** Anmeldung, permit card, health insurance, bank, integration course (1-year claim), driving licence (6 months) – two legal clocks she probably does not know about.
4. **Zed corner on the home screen.** Greeting in her language, Lusaka time and weather, Zambian holidays, radio and news – answers "stranded" directly and makes the app hers.
5. **Integration course guide.** Am I entitled or obliged, what it costs, exemption, nearest providers, what the two tests look like – course access is confusing in 2026.
6. **Five-minute daily German tied to her week.** Phrases for the thing she will actually do next (bus, Rewe, doctor), spaced repetition, A1 to A2.
7. **Help and safety page.** 112, 110, 116 117, women's helpline 116 016, anti-discrimination advice, and a plain note that her residence is not solely in her husband's hands – quiet but vital.
8. **People and places near Dreieich.** Women's networks in Frankfurt, binational families' association, vhs, integration office, MBE advice – a route out of the flat.
9. **Zambian kitchen in Germany.** German word, what to ask, where to buy, substitutes – food is the fastest cure for homesickness.
10. **Winter and wellbeing.** Layering, daylight, vitamin D prompt ("ask your doctor"), hair and skin care in dry heated air.
11. **Money home.** Regulated providers for EUR→ZMW with mobile money, plus a comparison link.
12. **Work and recognition starter.** "You are allowed to work", recognition portal, German CV basics, Minijob explained.

## 5. Zambia-specific facts

- **Community size:** 830 Zambian citizens in Germany on 31 Dec 2024; average age 34.3, average stay 8.7 years, 37 % married (Malawi 385, Zimbabwe 4,630) [V] Destatis. Naturalised Zambians are not counted. No figure for Hessen found.
- **Associations:** a Zambia German Association (ZGA) is named in embassy news and hosted an Independence party in Hamburg in 2022; embassy-led Independence events took place in Berlin (2024) and Stuttgart (2025) [S] ([embassy news](https://www.zambiaembassy.de/zambians-in-germany-encouraged-to-participate-in-national-development/)). **No website and no Rhein-Main group found** – ask the embassy.
- **Embassy:** Axel-Springer-Str. 54A, 10117 Berlin, +49 30 2062940, Mon–Fri 9–17 [V] ([site](https://www.zambiaembassy.de/contact/)). Services: passports (forms A and N), registration of Zambians, document legalisation, birth/marriage certificates. Honorary consul for Hessen in Bad Camberg [V]. The embassy's own holiday page dates from 2018 – do not use it.
- **Driving licence:** Zambia is **not** in Anlage 11 FeV [V]. The licence is valid 6 months from taking up residence (§29 FeV); afterwards she needs the German theory and practical tests. Compulsory driving-school training hours do not apply (§31(2) FeV) [V], but a driving school is needed to book and drive the test; eye test, first-aid course and a licence translation are also required [K]. Theory test is available in English [K]. Zambia drives on the left.
- **Time:** Zambia is CAT (UTC+2) all year. German winter (CET): Zambia +1 h; German summer (CEST): same time [K].
- **Flights:** no nonstop Frankfurt–Lusaka. One-stop options confirmed in sources: Ethiopian via Addis Ababa, Qatar via Doha, Emirates via Dubai; typical journey 11–14 h [S] ([Ethiopian](https://www.ethiopianairlines.com/en-de/flights-from-frankfurt-to-lusaka), Kayak/Skyscanner route pages). Via Johannesburg, Nairobi or Istanbul: not confirmed.
- **Holidays:** the 18 October National Day of Prayer was cancelled in October 2026 and merged into a new holiday on 29 December [S] ([Lusaka Times](https://www.lusakatimes.com/2026/10/09/govt-cancels-october-18-as-public-holiday/), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_holidays_in_Zambia)). Kenneth Kaunda Day (28 April) has existed since 2021.
- **Money:** Germany→Zambia confirmed on provider pages for Remitly (MTN, Airtel, Zamtel, banks), WorldRemit (MTN/Airtel, per-transfer caps), Sendwave, Western Union, Wise; Taptap Send lists both countries but the corridor itself was not confirmed [V/S].
